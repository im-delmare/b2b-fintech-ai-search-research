
# B2B Fintech Questions in AI Search

An exploratory research project documenting how ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity respond to questions relevant to B2B fintech buyers.

## Research objective

This study explores how AI answer engines address practical questions that fintech founders, product leaders, payment specialists and other B2B decision-makers may ask when researching financial technology providers and infrastructure.

The objective is to examine the information presented in AI-generated answers, the entities and providers mentioned, the sources cited, and the types of information that may influence a B2B buyer's evaluation.

## Research date

9 November 2026

## AI platforms

- ChatGPT
- Gemini
- Perplexity

## Research approach

The research consists of exploratory observations of AI-generated responses to fintech-related B2B questions.

The original queries and responses are treated as primary research records. Where available, the dataset documents cited URLs, mentioned entities and relevant observations.

Unavailable information, including model versions or interface details, is not inferred.

## Scope and limitations

This is an exploratory snapshot, not a controlled benchmark.

The observations do not establish:

- How AI engines work internally.
- Which fintech providers generate the most qualified leads.
- Whether a particular GEO strategy increases conversions.
- Whether the same responses will be reproduced in future runs.
- A universal ranking of fintech providers or information sources.

A citation or provider mention is an observation, not evidence of commercial preference or lead generation.

## Research materials

The repository is intended to contain:

- Research methodology and test documentation.
- Original queries and AI-generated responses.
- Structured records of cited sources and mentioned entities.
- Comparative observations and limitations.

## Why this research matters

B2B fintech buyers increasingly have access to AI-generated answers when investigating payment infrastructure, embedded finance, compliance, cross-border payments and other technical problems.

Understanding the information these systems present can help fintech companies identify content gaps, improve the clarity of their technical information and develop more useful resources for potential business customers.

## Documentation and dataset

- [Research methodology](research/methodology.md): study design, sample, recording approach and limitations.
- [Observations dataset](data/observations.jsonl): 30 captured responses across 10 B2B fintech questions and three AI platforms, in JSON Lines format.

The dataset preserves the captured response text. Model versions and interface details were not recorded in the source transcript and are left unspecified. See the methodology for the full limitations.
